      problem : value is not a number

      problem : three values instead of two
      caught  : PiezoValidationError: freq_range must be a tuple or list of 2 numbers (min_freq, max_freq).

      problem : a single number instead of a (min, max) pair
      caught  : PiezoValidationError: freq_range must be a tuple or list of 2 numbers (min_freq, max_freq).
